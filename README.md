# Spark

## Purpose
Spark is not your ordinary application for passing time. It is a dedicated idea management tool built for minds that are constantly generating new concepts, ensuring that no fleeting thought is ever lost. The purpose of Spark is to serve as a fast, reliable, and distraction-free sanctuary for capturing and evolving your ideas before they vanish.

## Problems Solved
Creative thinkers often lose their best ideas because they lack a dedicated place to quickly jot them down. Standard note-taking apps are cluttered with formatting tools, grocery lists, and unstructured text, making it difficult to find and focus on high-potential concepts. Spark solves this by providing a friction-free capture mechanism and a structured workflow specifically designed for brainstorming and curation.

## The Welcome Screen: Ignite Your Mind
From the moment you open Spark, you are greeted with a unique Welcome screen that challenges you. It requires you to actively "swipe to ignite" the application. This intentional interaction forces you to transition your mindset, making you realize that you are entering a space meant for serious ideation and creativity, not passive scrolling.

## Features
* **Quick Capture:** Instantly capture your sparks of inspiration as soon as they arrive with a single tap.
* **Pin and Discard Workflows:** Swipe right to pin ideas you want to keep visible, or swipe left to discard ideas into a dedicated graveyard.
* **Brainstorming Mode:** Expand upon your initial sparks in a focused brainstorming screen where you can add detailed descriptions and flesh out your concepts.
* **Intelligent Background Nudges:** The application monitors your activity. If an idea sits untouched for more than 7 days, or if you simply need a morning prompt to get thinking, Spark sends varied, unpredictable notifications to keep your mind engaged.
* **Graveyard System:** Discarded ideas are never permanently deleted by default. You can visit the graveyard to permanently delete or revive concepts you previously threw away.
